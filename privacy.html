Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Rahmaya-Banks International Construction ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it when you visit our website or submit inquiries.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when filling out our contact forms, requesting project estimates, or communicating with us directly. This includes:

• Full Name
• Email Address
• Phone Number
• Project details, addresses, or specifications provided in messages

3. Automatically Collected Data & Cookies

When you browse our website, our hosting providers and analytical tools may automatically log standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, and pages visited. We may use cookies to improve site performance and user experience.

4. How We Use Your Information

The data we collect is used strictly for internal business purposes, including:

• Responding to project inquiries and scheduling construction or remodeling consultations.
• Providing accurate service estimates and project proposals.
• Improving our website structure, design, and user experience.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We may only share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website or conducting our business, provided they agree to keep this information confidential.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and administrative security measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to review or delete your submitted information, please reach out via our Contact Page.
